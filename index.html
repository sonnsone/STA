The Swiss Tea Association brings together tea enthusiasts and tea professionals in Switzerland. The association exists to let us share our passion and knowledge, and to foster gatherings over a cup of tea. We also wish to promote tea and its world by visiting producers, mainly in Asia.

瑞士茶协会旨在汇聚瑞士的茶爱好者和茶行业专业人士。协会的宗旨是让我们能够分享对茶的热爱和知识，并促进大家围绕一杯茶相聚交流。我们还希望走访茶叶生产者（主要在亚洲），推广茶及茶文化。

You can contact us here: welcome@swisstea-association.ch
